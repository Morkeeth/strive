# Code of conduct

STRIVE welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful, specific and patient. Ask questions, explain decisions, credit other people's work, and critique the change rather than the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, spam, impersonation and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces.

If a public discussion needs moderation, use GitHub's Report content control on the affected message. Keep private details out of public reports. For a security or private-data problem, use the reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove harmful content, ask someone to change their behaviour, restrict participation or ban repeated or serious misconduct. They consider the conduct and impact, and avoid repeating private information in public moderation notes. No response time is promised.

Contributing does not require agreement on every technical choice. Disagreement must stay respectful and focused on the work.
